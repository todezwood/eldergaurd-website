How It Works

Listens to Calls

ElderGuard pays attention to phone calls as they happen. It listens for the words and patterns that scammers use over and over.

Checks for Danger

When a call sounds like a scam, ElderGuard sends a warning right away. It looks for common tricks like fake prizes, urgent payment demands, and people pretending to be someone they are not.

Protects Your Family

You get an alert when a call seems dangerous. That way you can step in, talk to your loved one, and help before any harm is done.

Who It Is For

For Elders

If you are a senior who gets a lot of scam calls, ElderGuard watches out for you. It is simple to set up and works quietly in the background. You do not need to be good with technology.

For Families

If you worry about a parent or grandparent getting scammed, ElderGuard gives you a way to know when something is wrong. You get alerts so you can help before it is too late.

For Anyone Who Is Worried

Scams do not only target seniors. If you or someone you know has been getting suspicious calls, ElderGuard can help you feel safer.

About

ElderGuard started with a simple idea: our elders deserve to feel safe when they answer the phone.

I am Navajo, and in my culture, elders are the ones we turn to for guidance and wisdom. They took care of us. Now it is our turn to take care of them. Scams target older people more than anyone else, and too many families have watched someone they love get taken advantage of. That should not happen.

So I built ElderGuard. It listens to calls, spots the tricks scammers use, and warns you and your family before any damage is done. It is not complicated. It is just looking out for the people who looked out for us.

In Development

We are building ElderGuard right now. The app is not ready yet, but we are working hard to get it to you. If you want to know when it is available, we will share updates as we get closer.
